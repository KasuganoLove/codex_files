## Conversion and reuse

The authors' complete single-page figure PDFs were rasterized with PyMuPDF at 2× scale in both axes, then encoded as lossless WebP. These copies contain only a format conversion: no added or removed panels, crop, relabeling, or generative editing. Decoded WebP pixels were independently verified against the original PNG rendering and a fresh full-page source-PDF rendering. Original comparison labels remain intact.
